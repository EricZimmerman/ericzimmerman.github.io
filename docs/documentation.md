## 🐞 Problems and ideas

Found a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue in [EricZimmerman/Issues](https://github.com/EricZimmerman/Issues/issues/new/choose),
which has templates for bug reports and feature requests and covers every tool, or in the tool's own repository.
Data that shows the problem is the fastest way to a fix.
